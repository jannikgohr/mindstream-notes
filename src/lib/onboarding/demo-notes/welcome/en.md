# Welcome to Mindstream

Create your first note with the new-note button in the sidebar and start writing. Your changes are saved automatically by default.

- [Import, export, and backup](demo:import-export) explains how to bring your notes with you and keep a copy.
- [Tips for everyday notes](demo:tips) covers shortcuts, links, and editor choices.
