# Welcome to Mindstream

This is your note. Edit it, replace it, or delete it whenever you like.

## Make it yours

- Create a note with the new-note button.
- Search your notes, or run any action from the command palette. **Settings > Hotkeys** lists the keys for both.
- Type `[[` to link to another note.
- Organize your notes into folders in the sidebar.

## Keep your notes safe

Your changes are saved automatically by default. Use **Settings > Data & Backup** to make a backup or import existing notes. You can connect a sync account in **Settings > Account & Sync**.

You can change your theme, editor view, and shortcuts in Settings, or run setup again under General.
